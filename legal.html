Who runs this site

The German Funding Navigator is a free, non commercial educational project by Jeel Swami, a private individual. You can reach me by email at jeel.swami@outlook.com or through GitHub issues, which is also the best place for corrections.

This site is not affiliated with, and not endorsed by, the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft, the European Research Council, the European Commission, EURAXESS, or any other organization named on it. It summarizes their public rules for educational purposes and links to every source. It is general information, not legal advice and not an official assessment; funding decisions rest entirely with the funders.

Privacy

The honest summary: this site collects nothing about you, and nothing you enter ever reaches me.

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address and technical access data for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in delivering the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); GitHub applies the recognized safeguards for transfers to the USA.
  • No tracking. No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no user accounts, no forms that send data anywhere.
  • The eligibility navigator runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are processed locally and never transmitted.
  • Local storage. Your checklist ticks and theme choice are saved in your browser's local storage because you set them. That storage is strictly necessary for a feature you explicitly use (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG), stays on your device, and disappears when you clear your browser data.
  • Your rights. The GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and complaint to a supervisory authority. Since this site holds no data about you, questions about the hosting logs are best directed to GitHub via its privacy statement.
